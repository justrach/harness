# Harness v0.2.157

A fix for macOS, Windows and Linux, and a new iOS build on TestFlight.

## The work pane stays closed until you open it

- In v0.2.156 the Working tree pane opened by itself whenever an agent ran a command, and came back on every turn and whenever you clicked another split pane. It now opens only when you ask: the running pill and the "N files changed" card still open it in one click.

## Agent questions read as they were written

- A question from an agent shows its formatting, such as bold, `code` and lists, as a reply does, instead of the raw asterisks and backticks.

## iPhone Duo lying flat

- With a session open on the inner display lying flat, the session list stays out of the way and the chat has the whole display. The sidebar button in the session's bar brings the list back beside it, and the session title is no longer cut off under the back button.
