# Harness v0.2.100-beta.2

Beta. Developer ID signed and notarized. Everything in
[v0.2.100-beta.1](v0.2.100-beta.1.md) is included. This is a prerelease for
testing: stable installs and the auto-updater do not see it.

## Continue with ChatGPT in Settings

- Settings → Accounts has a ChatGPT plan card. **Continue with ChatGPT** opens
  your browser to sign in, and OpenAI Codex then runs on your ChatGPT plan. The
  card shows **Using ChatGPT plan** with **Manage usage** and **Sign out**, and
  the first time it tells you how your plan is used.
- If you sign in but do not allow plan usage, the card says so and offers to
  sign in again.
- `harness chatgpt login`, `status` and `logout` still work.
- Not in yet: the plan indicator next to the model picker and a usage-limit
  message in the conversation. A single turn longer than an hour can fail when
  the sign-in token expires.

## Updating

This beta moves to the next stable release on its own. To move to a newer
beta, install its prerelease.
