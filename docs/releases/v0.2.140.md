# Harness v0.2.140

Desktop release for macOS, Windows and Linux, alongside the first iPhone build
with an Apple Watch app.

## Apple Watch

- Harness on the iPhone now includes a Watch app for speaking a message into
  a chat (#322). Tap **Speak**, talk, stop, review the text, then **Send**
  or **Discard**. Your iPhone delivers it like any message, so it shows up in
  the chat on every device.
- It goes to your most recent chat unless you pick another recent chat or
  **New chat**, which starts a plain chat on the Auto models. If the agent is
  working, choose **Queue** to wait for the turn to end or **Steer now**.
- **Type or dictate** uses the Watch's own dictation and keyboard.
- Recordings are written out on your iPhone, on the device where it
  supports that. Only the text is sent, as an ordinary message. The first
  recording asks for the speech-recognition permission on the iPhone.
- With the iPhone out of range, a message or recording waits on the Watch
  and is delivered once when they reconnect. The message you just sent shows
  at the top as waiting, sent, or why it couldn't be sent.
- A complication and Smart Stack widget open Harness on the Watch already
  recording.

## iPhone crash reports

- Settings → Diagnostics → **Crash reports** lists the crashes, hangs and
  heavy CPU or disk use iOS reported for Harness, usually on the launch
  after it happened. Reports stay on the iPhone until you share one. They
  hold code addresses, the build, the device model and the iOS version,
  never chat content.

The desktop apps have no changes of their own in this release. The Watch app
ships with the iPhone app through TestFlight and the App Store.
