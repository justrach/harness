# Harness v0.2.169

Split panes hold still when you click between them, equations are typeset, and a handful of fixes for panes, the sidebar, branches and the iOS link sheet.

## Desktop

- With the chat split side by side, clicking into another pane to type no longer moves the panes. The pane you were typing in used to be a pixel narrower per divider than the others, so every pane between the old focus and the new one shifted sideways, up to 7 pixels across eight panes. Every pane now keeps its width wherever the focus is. (#737)
- Equations in replies are typeset instead of flattened to plain text: fractions, roots, sub- and superscripts, matrices and annotated braces sit on the text's baseline in paragraphs, lists, headings and tables. Copying one copies its LaTeX source, an equation too wide for its pane scrolls inside it, and anything unsupported stays as its source text. (#621)
- A text selection that starts in one pane and ends in another no longer switches focus to the wrong pane, including when the chat or tab changed while the button was held. (#725)
- The sidebar no longer repeats New chat and Search chats as full-width rows; the titlebar + button, the sidebar's search icon and the keyboard shortcuts do the same thing, so your chats start higher up. (#712)
- The reasoning step of the model picker fits its controls instead of reserving a fixed height. (#696)
- When an agent creates and switches to a new branch itself (`git switch -c` or `git checkout -b`), your next message isn't held for a "Branch changed" confirmation any more. Harness checks that the agent's own successful command made exactly that branch before skipping it. (#734)

## iOS

- The link sheet fits its content instead of opening to a fixed half height with empty space below Copy link. (#696)
