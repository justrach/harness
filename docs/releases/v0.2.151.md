# Harness v0.2.151

Desktop update for macOS, Windows and Linux, and a new iOS build on TestFlight.

## A new model picker

- Models are grouped by provider, with a card beside each one that names how it is served, its reasoning range and how it is billed. Choosing a model opens a second step to set its reasoning on a slider that settles on the nearest level. This is Phil Hedayatnia's picker design.
- The picker shows only what each agent's catalog lists.
- Setting `HARNESS_MODEL_PICKER_PREVIEW=0` brings back the previous picker.

## iOS

- The composer follows the desktop's design: in light themes the writing surface is brighter, the caret and the edge while typing take your accent color, and Send and Stop are filled with the accent.
- On iPhone Duo the app uses the whole inner display: TestFlight builds are now made with Xcode 27.1.
