# Harness v0.2.149

Desktop update for macOS, Windows and Linux, and a new iOS build on TestFlight.

## The model picker sits under the composer

- The model and reasoning picker moves out of the writing box into the row beneath it, beside the project and branch, so the box holds only your message. This follows Phil Hedayatnia's composer design.

## A rounded edge where the conversation meets the sidebar

- With the sidebar open, the conversation column's corners round where it meets the sidebar, and the dividing line follows the curve. A collapsed sidebar keeps the straight edge.

## iOS

- On iPhone Duo lying flat, the app is the ordinary one-column iPhone app. Folded like a book, the session list and the open session sit on either side of the fold. iPad keeps the list beside the session.
