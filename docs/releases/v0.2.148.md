# Harness v0.2.148

Desktop update for macOS, Windows and Linux, and a new iOS build on TestFlight.

## A brighter composer and main screen

- The composer is a bright writing surface with soft shadows, a send button in your accent color, and an accent caret and edge while you type. This follows Phil Hedayatnia's composer design.
- In light themes the conversation column is lifted toward white, so the sidebar reads as the frame around it.

## Context savings, easier to read

- The composer footer shows only the context ring and its percentage.
- Hovering the ring shows how many tokens graff kept out of the context as one number with a meter, split into tool output kept in files and what compaction freed. Hovering a part of the meter gives its exact count.
- The footer cards (the context ring and the agent's steps) stay open while the pointer is on them, so their contents can be hovered and clicked.

## Annotate images before sending

- Click an image in the chat or the composer, then **Annotate**: draw numbered boxes with notes, or drop text labels. **Done** sends the marked-up image, with the numbered notes added to your prompt.

## Queue or steer a follow-up

- While the agent is working, the send button becomes **Queue** or **Steer**. Queue sends your message after the current turn; Steer hands it to the running turn at its next safe point. The chevron beside it switches between them, and the message shows in the transcript right away.

## Smaller fixes

- Images in an agent's reply no longer flash between loaded and "Loading image…" when several large ones are on screen.
- A build waiting in the queue shows an animated waiting status.
- The first time you close a chat with ⌘W, Harness remembers your choice and explains where to change it.
- New worktree branches are named after their task.
- ⌘F reveals and focuses the session search.
- The worktree and pull request actions in the footer no longer overlap.

## iOS

- Home reads like a messages list: agent avatars, larger session names, and no row dividers.
- Status chips (Working, Needs you, Failed) use a soft tint of their color instead of a solid fill.
- On iPhone Duo, the session list and the open session sit on either side of the fold.
