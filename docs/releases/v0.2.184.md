# Harness v0.2.184

## Highlights

- **An agent's question stays inside its pane (`apps/nano/src/view_question.zig`, `apps/nano/src/view_composer.zig`, `apps/nano/src/motion_rules.zig`, `apps/nano/lean-proofs/Nano/QuestionPanel.lean`)**:
  - A long question in a split or short pane no longer pushes its options, the answer box and `Submit` below the pane's edge. The panel takes at most the room its pane has; the question and the options scroll in what the header, the answer box and the buttons leave, the question giving way first so the choices stay in view (`split_within_budget`, `split_question_yields_first`, `panel_ends_inside_pane`). A question that scrolls on fades at its foot.
  - The question's chip in the transcript ends inside its column in a narrow pane instead of running past it and losing `Waiting for you` (`chip_within_column`).
- **Draft composer footer in split panes (`apps/nano/src/view_newchat.zig`, `apps/nano/lean-proofs/Nano/FooterChips.lean`)**:
  - The controls under a new chat's composer (`Code`, `Steer`, `Run on: …`, project, checkout, ref) are no longer cut at the composer's edge in a split pane. Long labels shorten, then controls keep their glyph alone with the label in a tooltip, the ref last; none is partly cut or pushed out of reach (`never_overflows`, `footer_conservation_holds`, `footer_split_windows_hold`).
- **A message sent while a turn works sits where it arrived (`apps/nano/src/steer_order.zig`, `apps/nano/src/view_transcript.zig`)**:
  - A steered message is drawn at the point in the running reply where it was sent, with the turn's later work under it, instead of staying last while new work piles up above it. Sending a steer scrolls the transcript to its end, as any send does.
- **Zen and Developer modes (`apps/nano/src/settings.zig`, `apps/nano/src/view_sidebar_menus.zig`, `apps/nano/src/palette.zig`, `apps/ios/Harness/Views/HomeListControls.swift`, `apps/nano/lean-proofs/Nano/FrameSchedule.lean`)**:
  - Zen, the default, is a flat session list without the open-workspaces tree, multi-axis filters, row badges, git checkout controls, the prompt rail and the action toolbar; Developer shows them all. `Cmd+Shift+M`, the command palette and the sidebar menu switch between the two on the desktop, and each part can be turned on or off on its own. iOS has the same presets and switches in its list controls and Settings.
- **graff Bots from a chat (`crates/mcp/src/tools/bots.rs`, `docs/mcp.md`)**:
  - The Harness MCP server gains `list_bots`, `run_bot` and `run_due_bots`: a project's bots and their routines, and a run of one as an ordinary graff chat in its project. Nothing calls `run_due_bots` on a timer yet.
- **Edge request limits (`edge/src/body.ts`, `edge/src/releases.ts`)**:
  - `GET /releases/<malformed %-escape>` answers 400 instead of failing as a Worker exception.
  - The device sidecar, session diff and room wake-ack uploads have size limits (4 MiB, 24 MiB, 64 KiB), and the tool blob, chat2 checkpoint, chat2 sidecar and vault uploads refuse an oversized body while reading it rather than after. `?token=` is dropped from the URL forwarded to chat2 and registry rooms.
  - `jose` 6.2.17 and in-range test tooling updates.
