# Harness v0.2.100-beta.1

Beta. Developer ID signed and notarized. Everything in [v0.2.99](v0.2.99.md) is
included. This is a prerelease for testing: stable installs and the
auto-updater do not see it.

## Run OpenAI Codex on your ChatGPT plan

- `harness chatgpt login` opens your browser to sign in to ChatGPT. Once you
  approve it, OpenAI Codex sessions run on your ChatGPT plan, and the model
  list is your account's own.
- `harness chatgpt status` shows whether you are signed in and whether Codex
  is on your plan. `harness chatgpt logout` ends the session.
- The sign-in stays on your computer. Set `HARNESS_CHATGPT_PLAN=0` to keep
  Codex off your plan while signed in; stable releases keep it off until it is
  promoted.
- Not in yet: a button in the app (use the command), and a single turn longer
  than an hour can fail when the sign-in token expires.

## Updating

This beta moves to the next stable release on its own. To move to a newer
beta, install its prerelease.
