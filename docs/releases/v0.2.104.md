# Harness v0.2.104

Stable patch release for macOS, Windows and Linux. Includes the changes in
[v0.2.103](v0.2.103.md).

## Reconnect ChatGPT without leaving the chat

When a chat stops because its ChatGPT sign-in expired, the error now carries
everything you need to get going again. You no longer have to find the right
account in Settings.

- **Reconnect ChatGPT** on the error starts the sign-in on the computer the
  chat runs on, even when you're looking at it from another device. The card
  shows each step: starting, waiting for approval on that computer, failed
  (with **Try again**) and **ChatGPT reconnected**.
- **Resume conversation** appears once the sign-in is confirmed. It continues
  the same chat with its saved agent and model, and only when you click it.
  Nothing is resent on its own: the prompt that failed and the tools that
  already finished are not replayed, and your unsent draft and attachments
  are left alone. Resume waits while a turn is running and is offered once.
- **One sign-in per computer and account.** If several chats hit the same
  expired sign-in, or you open the error from your phone too, they all join
  the one approval already waiting instead of starting new ones or cancelling
  it. Closing a card only stops watching; only **Cancel** ends the sign-in.
- **Recover from your iPhone.** For the Codex ChatGPT sign-in, a chat on your
  computer can be reconnected from the phone with a one-time sign-in code, and
  the renewed sign-in is stored on the computer that runs the chat. The new
  ChatGPT plan sign-in finishes in the browser on that computer, and the phone
  shows "Complete sign-in on" that computer while it waits.
- Errors the chat has already moved past show no buttons, so old errors
  higher up don't invite you to sign in again.
- Clear messages when recovery can't run: the chat's computer is offline,
  didn't respond, or needs a newer Harness ("Update Harness on the chat's
  computer to reconnect from here"), or the chat has no saved agent to resume
  with.
- A neutral note explains that a recent password reset or Advanced Account
  Security enrollment (including Daybreak) can make ChatGPT ask you to sign in
  again. Harness doesn't guess whether that happened, and never suggests
  turning account security off.

Harness can't keep alive a sign-in that ChatGPT itself has ended. This
release makes signing back in a one-click step from the chat.

## Agents working with other chats

- A chat that messages another chat and waits for its answer no longer sits
  on a silent tool call for up to an hour. From a chat, `send_message`,
  `wait_for_turn` and `create_chat` now wait at most two minutes. When the
  wait runs out, the agent is told the other chat will reply into its own
  chat, so it ends its turn instead of waiting again. Scripts and tools that
  aren't a chat keep the longer limit.

## Cloud sandboxes

For accounts signed in with Codegraff:

- **Settings → Accounts → Cloud sandboxes** lists your sandboxes with their
  state (for example "Running · Harness ready · pauses in 24 min") and
  **Create**, **Start**, **Stop** and **Delete** (with a "Delete for good?"
  confirmation). Sandbox use comes from your Codegraff credit.
- **Create** makes a persistent sandbox and sets Harness up in it, so it joins
  your device list like any other computer. **Start** wakes a paused sandbox
  and sets Harness up again; **Set up Harness** repairs one that is running
  without it.
- The **device picker** offers the same: **New cloud sandbox** when you have
  none, **Wake cloud sandbox** for a paused one. It shows progress (Creating,
  Waking, Connecting) and selects the sandbox once it is online.
- Errors read as plain sentences, such as "The sandbox started but has not
  shown up as a device yet. Try again in a moment."
- Set `HARNESS_CLOUD_SANDBOXES=0` to hide cloud sandboxes.

## Devices and accounts

- **Settings → Devices → Remove** forgets an offline device. Harness won't
  remove this computer or a device that's online. A removed device that starts
  Harness again comes back by itself.
- The account menu has a single **Sign out** (Harness's). The Codegraff row
  opens Settings → Accounts, where the Codegraff card has its own Sign out
  next to its usage and sandboxes.

## Linux

- Release tarballs are built on Ubuntu 22.04, so they start on Debian 12 and
  other systems with glibc 2.35 or newer.

## iPhone (ships separately)

These changes are in the iPhone app source and reach phones through their
own build, not this desktop release:

- The same Reconnect ChatGPT card and Resume conversation, with a sign-in
  code for the Codex sign-in.
- Typing in a stacked split's message box gives the session the screen above
  the keyboard. Sending puts the keyboard away and brings the split back.
- The split handle has a third resting spot: drag it down to give the list the
  whole screen, and the handle waits at the bottom to bring the session back.
- The status bar and home-indicator strips are colored again. On iOS 27.1
  and later they showed plain white.

Terminal-client packages are distributed separately from this desktop
release.
