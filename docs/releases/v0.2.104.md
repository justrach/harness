# Harness v0.2.104

Stable patch release for macOS, Windows and Linux. Includes the changes in
[v0.2.103](v0.2.103.md).

## Agents

- When ChatGPT needs you to sign in again, you can reconnect right in the
  chat. The card signs in on the computer the chat runs on, shows when it is
  waiting for your approval, and lets you cancel or retry. Your draft and the
  conversation stay where they are.
- Asking to reconnect from a second chat, or from your phone, joins the sign-in
  that is already waiting instead of cancelling it. Only Cancel ends it.
- Once you're reconnected, Resume conversation continues the same chat when you
  click it. Nothing is resent automatically: the message that failed and the
  tools that already ran are not repeated.

## Cloud sandboxes

- Settings → Accounts lists your Codegraff cloud sandboxes, with Create, Start,
  Stop and Delete. A sandbox runs Harness and appears with your devices; its
  use comes from your Codegraff credit.
- The device picker offers to create a cloud sandbox, or to wake a paused one,
  and selects it once it is online.

## Devices and account

- Settings → Devices can remove a device that is offline, such as a finished
  sandbox or a computer you no longer use. It comes back by itself if it starts
  Harness again.
- The account menu has one Sign out. Signing out of Codegraff now lives in
  Settings → Accounts.

Mobile app and terminal-client packages are distributed separately from this
desktop release.
